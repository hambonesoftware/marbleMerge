GEOMETRY & PHYSICS REVIEW

A trumpet that works like a trumpet.

The corrected race uses a steeper throat, a real discharge neck, and a receiving track with room for merged marbles. This diagram plots the actual before-and-after surface definitions; it is not a gameplay screenshot.

Funnel cross-section

Dashed: previous bowlSolid: corrected trumpet + neck

The new profile changes from approximately 39° at the mouth to 75° at the throat. A low collision rim leaves an opening at the track entrance. The outlet ends above the receiving channel so larger marbles can clear it.

Validation

Three full races exercise the same course builders, contacts, progress tracking and merge rules used by the playable page. All complete with finishers and no stalled survivors; fall-offs remain possible. Individual regression checks cover mass, velocity, spin and axle reset.

Run the live-rules diagnostic

The cloud test browser cannot render WebGL. Geometry and physics checks do not replace an iPhone visual/performance check. A complete photographic comparison of every obstacle is still outstanding.
